      ₹25 is a non-refundable game entry fee once payment is confirmed. No refund for absence, wrong answer, elimination, disqualification, change of mind, or participant-side device/network problems. If 500 verified slots are not reached for that Sunday, the paid entry remains valid and is automatically carried forward to the next eligible Sunday quiz; no second entry fee is required.

      Payment must be verified before a slot is reserved. Once verified, the ₹25 entry fee is treated as paid entry credit for the quiz and is non-refundable, except where a payment reversal is required by applicable law or the payment provider.
      ⏱️ Server CountdownEvery question has a server-controlled time limit. Late answers are rejected automatically.
      ❌ One Wrong = EliminatedWrong answer or no answer before countdown ends removes the participant from the next round.
      ⚡ Response TimeAfter each round, each participant sees their own response time plus active/correct/eliminated counts. Admin sees complete timing records.
      🛡️ Fair PlayOne verified entry = one live session. Server timestamps, randomized option order, session heartbeat and tab-switch monitoring reduce cheating.
      🏁 Tie BreakIf multiple players survive the final round, lowest cumulative correct-response time ranks first; an exact tie can go to sudden death.
      🔁 Weekly PrizesPrize values and bonus gifts are admin-configurable every week before registration opens.
